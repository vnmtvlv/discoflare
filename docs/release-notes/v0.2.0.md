# Discoflare 0.2.0

Every conversation gets its own Live room, and the new **Discoflare Admin** runs your workspaces from inside your own Cloudflare account.

## What's new

- **Discoflare Admin.** One small Worker per Cloudflare account creates, updates, and removes your workspaces, connects their domains and email, and provides Live. It is the only place in the account that holds a Cloudflare credential; workspaces reach it through a service binding and hold none. It updates itself, and can keep your workspaces up to date too. discoflare.com only deploys it and lists it, and keeps no Cloudflare credential.
- **A Live room in every conversation.** Every channel and direct message has one room for audio, camera, and screen sharing; there are no separate voice channels. A 1:1 direct message rings as a **Call**: it can be declined and stops ringing after 45 seconds. Channels and group DMs announce a room once and let people drop in.
- **Hosts and participants.** Whoever starts a room, and channel managers, are its hosts and can remove people or end it for everyone. Everyone else joins without those controls.
- **Start live sessions** is now the grant name, and it applies in direct messages too, so you can make a role that joins calls but never starts them.
- **Access follows the conversation.** Removing someone from the workspace, a private channel, or a group DM takes them out of its live room and revokes their access to it.
- **Live without setup.** Workspaces linked to a Discoflare Admin need no RealtimeKit configuration. Other installations connect Live with a single Cloudflare API token; Discoflare creates its own RealtimeKit app and presets.
- **Mail.** The conversation list can be resized, and emails take less space.

## Changes

- Voice channels are retired. Existing ones become ordinary text channels and keep their messages; new installations no longer create one.
- Scheduling live sessions is removed until Discoflare has a calendar.
- Starting a live session no longer waits in a loop: errors such as a missing permission or a rate limit show at once.
- Deployment variables are now `REALTIMEKIT_API_TOKEN`, `REALTIMEKIT_PRESET_HOST`, and `REALTIMEKIT_PRESET_PARTICIPANT`; the older `REALTIMEKIT_API_KEY` and `REALTIMEKIT_PRESET_AV` are still read. The Basic Auth RealtimeKit API (`REALTIMEKIT_API_SECRET`) and revealing a saved token are removed.

## Deployment

- One database migration: voice channels become text channels, and RealtimeKit settings split host and participant presets. Workspaces connected before the split keep sharing one preset until the owner selects **Reconnect** in **Workspace Settings → Live**.
- Workspaces installed from discoflare.com keep working as before. To move one to the Discoflare Admin, deploy an Admin to its Cloudflare account from discoflare.com, then select **Update** for it in the Admin; its domains and email move over without reconnecting.
- Releases now publish the Discoflare Admin (`discoflare-admin.mjs`, `discoflare-admin-assets.json`) and `@discoflare/admin-core` next to the workspace in the same manifest.
- Repository deployments that build from source need Node.js 24, as pinned in `.node-version`.

## Known limitations

- Who is in a live room is tracked by heartbeats from each browser; a tab that is throttled in the background for over a minute can briefly appear to have left.
- Deleting a workspace still starts in **Workspace Settings → Danger zone**.
