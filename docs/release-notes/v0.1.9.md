# Discoflare 0.1.9

A Sent folder for Mail, and a one-click fix for email domains that could receive mail but not send it.

## What's new

- **Sent folder.** Every mailbox now has **Sent**, listing the conversations you have sent email in, most recently sent first. Conversations in Spam or Trash are left out.
- **Inbox shows mail sent to you.** Like Gmail, a conversation you start lives in Sent and moves into the Inbox once someone replies. Before, every email you started appeared in the Inbox straight away. Archiving a conversation keeps it in Sent.
- **Repair sending.** **Workspace Settings → Email → Domains** marks a domain that receives mail but cannot send it and offers **Repair sending** to the owner. Repair finishes the domain's setup in Cloudflare; mailboxes and routing are not changed.

## Fixes

- A new email that fails to send now says "Your email was saved but not delivered" instead of calling it a reply.
- After Compose, the new conversation opens in Sent.

## Deployment

- There are no database migrations in this release.
- Guided installations: email domains connected at the top level of a zone, such as `example.com` rather than `mail.example.com`, were set up for receiving but not for sending. The discoflare.com installer now sets them up for both. Domains connected before that fix show **Receives mail but cannot send it** in Email settings; select **Repair sending** once for each.
- Existing managed workspaces can update from **Workspace Settings → System → Updates**.
- Repository deployments that build from source need Node.js 24, as pinned in `.node-version`.

## Known limitations

- Conversations cannot yet be moved back to the Inbox from Archive, Spam, or Trash.
