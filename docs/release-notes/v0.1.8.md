# Discoflare 0.1.8

Mail that keeps up with you, email that stays in the mailbox it was sent to, and a fix for records lost while typing into a new database.

## What's new

- **Mail opens instantly.** Hovering a conversation loads it, and opening one shows its subject and people straight away. Replies and internal notes appear as soon as you send them. Archive, Spam, and Trash move the conversation at once, and unread counts update as you read.
- **New mail arrives live.** Open mail views update as email arrives, replies are sent, or conversations move, instead of checking every 15 seconds.
- **See whether an email was delivered.** Sent email shows **Sending**, **Sent**, or **Not delivered** with the reason. An email that was not delivered can be sent again with **Retry**.
- **Older conversations.** A mailbox lists 50 conversations at a time with **Load older conversations**. Before, conversations beyond the newest 200 could not be reached.
- **Faster chat and mail loading.** Messages load their authors, reactions, pins, attachments, and replies together instead of one after another.

## Fixes

- Records typed quickly into the New record row of a newly created database could be lost. Every record is now saved in the order it was typed, and the field keeps focus.
- One email sent to two of your mailboxes now arrives in both. It was only kept in the first.
- A reply can no longer join a conversation in a different mailbox. Each mailbox only threads its own email.
- A conversation keeps everyone who took part. Before, each new email replaced the list with only its own people. Replies go to the sender, To, and Cc of the latest email received.
- Email that failed to send was shown as Sent. It now shows as Not delivered.
- Retrying a send after a dropped connection no longer risks sending the email twice.
- Email larger than 25 MB is rejected. Up to 20 attachments of up to 10 MB each are kept, and the message names any attachments that were left out.
- Hovering or reloading a conversation no longer marks it read; reading it does.

## Deployment

- This release adds database migration `0028_mailbox_scoped_email_delivery`. Guided installations and `pnpm deploy` apply it automatically. It records which mailbox each email belongs to and adds delivery details to sent email.
- **Downgrading is not supported.** 0.1.7 does not record which mailbox an email belongs to, so email handled after a downgrade would not thread or de-duplicate correctly once you update again. If you must roll back, restore a backup taken before updating.
- There are no new Cloudflare bindings or permissions.
- Existing managed workspaces can update from **Workspace Settings → System → Updates**.
- Repository deployments that build from source need Node.js 24, as pinned in `.node-version`.

## Known limitations

- A failed email is retried only when someone selects Retry; there is no automatic retry yet.
- Compose supports To, Subject, and plain text. CC, attachments, and drafts are not available yet.
- The 0.1.7 notes said code blocks show a language label. The language is recognized, but no label is shown yet.
